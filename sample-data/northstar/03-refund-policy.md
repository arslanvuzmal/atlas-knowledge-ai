# Northstar Cloud Refund and Cancellation Policy

This policy is fictional and exists to demonstrate Atlas Knowledge AI. It describes no real company's terms and creates no obligation.

## Summary

Monthly subscriptions may be refunded in full within 14 days of the first payment. Annual subscriptions may be refunded in full within 30 days of the first payment. After those windows, payments are non-refundable except where this policy or applicable law states otherwise.

## Refund Window for Monthly Subscriptions

A customer on a monthly subscription may request a full refund within 14 days of their first payment. The refund covers that first payment only.

Refunds are not available on monthly renewals after the first payment. A customer who no longer wishes to continue should cancel before the next renewal date; the subscription then remains active until the end of the paid period.

## Refund Window for Annual Subscriptions

A customer on an annual subscription may request a full refund within 30 days of their first annual payment.

The 30-day window applies to the first annual payment only. It does not reset when an annual subscription renews.

After the 30-day window, an annual subscription cannot be refunded on a pro rata basis. The subscription remains active until the end of the paid term, after which it can be cancelled without further charge.

## Refunds After the Refund Window

Outside the stated windows, Northstar Cloud issues refunds in three circumstances.

Where a customer was charged after cancelling, the incorrect charge is refunded in full.

Where a duplicate charge was taken for the same billing period, the duplicate is refunded in full.

Where a confirmed platform outage exceeded the uptime commitment on a Business or Enterprise plan, a service credit is applied to the following invoice. Service credits are applied as credit against future invoices and are not paid out as cash.

## Non-Refundable Items

Overage charges for Flow runs already consumed are not refundable, because the runs were executed and the underlying cost was incurred.

Seats removed part-way through a billing period are not refunded for the remainder of that period.

Professional services, including migration assistance and custom Connector development, are not refundable once the work has begun.

## How to Request a Refund

Refund requests are submitted from the Billing page of the Northstar Cloud console using the Request Refund action, or by emailing the billing team.

A request must include the workspace name and the invoice number. Requests are acknowledged within 1 business day and a decision is issued within 5 business days.

An approved refund is returned to the original payment method. Card refunds typically appear within 5 to 10 business days depending on the issuing bank. Bank transfer refunds are processed within 10 business days.

## Cancellation

A subscription can be cancelled at any time from the Billing page. Cancellation does not require contacting support.

Cancelling stops future charges. The subscription remains fully active until the end of the period that has already been paid for. A monthly subscription cancelled on day 10 therefore continues to work until day 30.

Annual subscriptions cancelled mid-term continue to work until the end of the annual term and are not refunded for the unused months, unless the cancellation falls inside the 30-day refund window.

## What Happens to Data After Cancellation

When a subscription ends, the workspace becomes read-only. Flows stop running, and existing Flows and run history remain visible.

Workspace data is retained for 90 days after the subscription ends. During those 90 days, the subscription can be reactivated and everything resumes exactly as it was.

After 90 days, the workspace and all associated data are scheduled for permanent deletion, which completes within a further 30 days. Deletion is irreversible.

A full data export can be requested at any time before deletion, including during the read-only period. Exports are delivered as JSON and CSV within 5 business days.

## Enterprise Agreements

Where a signed Enterprise agreement contains refund or cancellation terms, those contractual terms take precedence over this policy. Enterprise customers should refer to their agreement or contact their customer success manager.

## Disputes

A customer who disagrees with a refund decision may escalate it in writing to the billing team, who will review the decision within 10 business days. This policy does not limit any statutory rights the customer has under the consumer law of their jurisdiction.
