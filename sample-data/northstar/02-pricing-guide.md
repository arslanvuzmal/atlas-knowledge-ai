# Northstar Cloud Pricing and Subscription Guide

All prices and terms in this document are fictional and exist to demonstrate Atlas Knowledge AI. They describe no real product or company.

## Plans

Northstar Cloud is sold on four plans: Starter, Team, Business, and Enterprise.

The Starter plan costs 29 US dollars per user per month. It includes 2,000 Flow runs per month, 7 days of run history, 10 Connectors, and email support.

The Team plan costs 79 US dollars per user per month. It includes 20,000 Flow runs per month, 30 days of run history, unlimited Connectors, scheduled triggers down to a five-minute interval, and priority email support.

The Business plan costs 149 US dollars per user per month. It includes 100,000 Flow runs per month, 180 days of run history, one-minute schedule intervals, single sign-on, audit log export, scheduled reports, and an uptime target of 99.9%.

The Enterprise plan is priced individually. It adds custom run volume, 365 days of run history, a 99.95% uptime commitment with service credits, a named customer success manager, data residency selection, and a formal security review.

## Billing Cycles

Subscriptions are billed either monthly or annually.

Annual billing is charged once at the start of the term and carries a 20% discount against the monthly price. On the Team plan this reduces the effective cost from 79 to 63.20 US dollars per user per month.

Monthly billing is charged on the same calendar day each month. Where that day does not exist in a shorter month, the charge falls on the last day of that month.

## Minimum Seats

Starter and Team have a minimum of 1 seat. Business has a minimum of 5 seats. Enterprise has a minimum of 25 seats.

## Adding and Removing Seats

Seats can be added at any time. A seat added mid-cycle is charged pro rata for the remainder of the current billing period, and the full rate applies from the next period.

Seats can be removed at any time, but the reduction takes effect at the start of the next billing period. Removing a seat does not generate a refund for the current period. This applies to both monthly and annual subscriptions.

## Overage

Flow runs beyond the plan allowance are billed as overage at the end of the billing period.

Overage costs 2 US cents per run on Starter and Team, and 1 US cent per run on Business. Enterprise overage rates are set in the contract.

A workspace owner receives an email notification when usage reaches 80% and again at 100% of the monthly allowance. Overage can be capped from the Billing page; when a cap is reached, Flow runs are paused until the next billing period begins or the cap is raised.

## Free Trial

Every new workspace begins with a 14-day free trial of the Team plan. No payment card is required to start the trial.

The trial includes the full Team feature set with a limit of 1,000 Flow runs. When the trial ends without a plan being selected, the workspace moves to the Starter plan and any Flows exceeding Starter limits are paused rather than deleted.

## Upgrades and Downgrades

An upgrade takes effect immediately. The remaining value of the current period is credited against the new plan, and the difference is charged at once.

A downgrade takes effect at the start of the next billing period. The current plan's features remain available until then. If the new plan has a lower seat count than the workspace currently uses, seats must be removed before the downgrade can be scheduled.

## Taxes

Prices exclude sales tax, VAT, and GST. Applicable tax is calculated at checkout from the billing address. Customers with a valid VAT registration number may enter it on the Billing page to apply the reverse charge where their jurisdiction allows it.

## Payment Methods

Northstar Cloud accepts Visa, Mastercard, and American Express on all plans. Business and Enterprise customers may pay by bank transfer against an invoice with 30-day payment terms. Invoicing is not available on Starter or Team.

## Failed Payments

When a payment fails, Northstar Cloud retries after 3 days, then after 7 days, then after 14 days.

If all retries fail, the workspace moves to a read-only state in which existing Flows are paused, run history remains visible, and no new runs are started. The workspace is restored immediately once payment succeeds. Data is retained in the read-only state for 90 days.

## Non-Profit and Education Pricing

Registered non-profit organisations and accredited educational institutions receive a 40% discount on the Team and Business plans. Eligibility is confirmed by the sales team before the discount is applied, and the discount cannot be combined with the annual billing discount.

## Price Changes

Published prices may change with 60 days' notice. An existing annual subscription keeps its agreed price until the end of the current term. An existing monthly subscription moves to the new price at the first billing date after the notice period ends.
