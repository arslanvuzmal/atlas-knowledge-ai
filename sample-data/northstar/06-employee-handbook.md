# Northstar Cloud Employee Handbook

ACCESS LEVEL: EMPLOYEE. This document is fictional and exists to demonstrate access-controlled retrieval in Atlas Knowledge AI. It describes no real employer and creates no employment terms.

## About This Handbook

This handbook applies to all Northstar Cloud employees. It is maintained by the People team and reviewed annually.

## Working Hours and Flexibility

Northstar Cloud operates a flexible working policy. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your registered working timezone, and employees are expected to be reachable during that window. Work outside core hours is arranged with your manager.

The standard working week is 37.5 hours. Employees are not expected to work evenings or weekends other than during a declared incident, and time worked during an incident outside normal hours is compensated with equivalent time off.

## Remote and Hybrid Work

Northstar Cloud is remote-first. Employees may work from any location within their contracted country of employment.

Working from another country requires prior approval from People and Finance, because it can create tax and employment obligations. Approved temporary work abroad is limited to 30 days in any rolling 12-month period.

Employees in a city with a Northstar Cloud office may use it freely and are not required to attend on any set number of days.

## Annual Leave

Employees receive 28 days of paid annual leave per year in addition to public holidays in their country of employment.

Leave is requested through the People system and requires manager approval. Requests of 5 days or more should be submitted at least 3 weeks in advance.

Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the following year and must be taken by 31 March. Days beyond 5 are forfeited.

## Sick Leave

Employees should notify their manager as early as possible on the first day of absence. No medical certificate is required for absences of up to 5 consecutive working days.

Absences longer than 5 consecutive working days require a certificate from a medical practitioner, submitted to People.

Company sick pay covers full salary for the first 20 working days of absence in any rolling 12-month period, after which statutory provisions in the country of employment apply.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 20 weeks of leave at full pay. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks at full pay. Both are available to employees regardless of gender and apply to birth, adoption, and surrogacy.

Eligibility begins after 6 months of continuous employment. Leave must begin within 12 months of the birth or placement and may be taken in up to three separate blocks.

## Equipment

Every employee receives a laptop, an external monitor, a keyboard, and a mouse. A one-time home office budget of 800 US dollars is available for a desk, a chair, and lighting.

Equipment remains the property of Northstar Cloud and must be returned when employment ends. Laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and the managed endpoint agent installed.

## Learning and Development

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 US dollars for courses, conferences, and books, approved by their manager.

Employees may use up to 4 hours per month of working time for self-directed learning.

## Expenses

Expenses are submitted through the Finance system within 30 days of being incurred, with a receipt attached. Expenses submitted more than 60 days after the fact require director approval.

Travel is booked through the company travel platform. Economy class is standard; flights over 8 hours may be booked in premium economy.

## Performance and Progression

Performance conversations happen quarterly. They are a two-way discussion covering objectives, growth, and support needed, and are not scored.

A formal progression review runs twice a year, in March and September. Compensation changes take effect the month after the review concludes.

## Security Obligations

Every employee must complete security awareness training within 14 days of joining and annually thereafter.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on every work account. Company credentials must be stored only in the approved password manager.

Customer data must never be copied to a personal device, a personal cloud account, or a local spreadsheet. Access to a customer workspace requires an approved support access grant and a recorded business reason.

Suspected security incidents must be reported immediately to the security team through the dedicated channel. Reporting a suspected incident that turns out to be benign is always the right decision and is never penalised.

## Code of Conduct

Northstar Cloud expects professional, respectful behaviour from every employee. Harassment, discrimination, and bullying are grounds for disciplinary action up to and including dismissal.

Concerns may be raised with a manager, with People, or through the confidential reporting line. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is itself a disciplinary matter.

## Leaving the Company

The standard notice period is 1 month for individual contributors and 3 months for director level and above, unless the employment contract states otherwise.

On the final day, all equipment is returned and all access is revoked. An optional exit conversation with People is offered and its content is confidential.
